# Content System — pillars, formulas, hooks

The strategy the posts are built on. You don't need to read this to use the engine —
it's here so the content stays on-brand when it's regenerated.

## Who we're talking to

1. **Wix site owners** (small business, solopreneurs) — want their site to do more without a developer.
2. **Wix agencies / freelancers** (e.g. Maciej, Spindogs) — want tools that make client sites better/faster. These are on **LinkedIn**.
3. **Other builders / indie hackers** — peers who amplify and try things. These are on **X**.

## Voice

- Plainspoken, a little dry, no hype, no emoji-soup. You genuinely dislike marketing fluff — lean into that. It reads as honest.
- Show, don't claim. A 6-second clip of DeepFrame beats "stunning animations!".
- British spelling. Sign as Pete / Digital Vision.

## The 4 content pillars (rotate these)

1. **Value / educational** — a Wix tip, a thing most people don't know their site can do. Earns reach because it's useful even if you never install the app. *(Most posts.)*
2. **Build-in-public** — "submitted DeepFrame to the App Market today, here's what the review wants." Honest progress. Builds the small loyal audience that actually converts.
3. **Product** — what the app does + a clear CTA. Used sparingly (1/week) so the feed isn't an advert.
4. **Visual** — DeepFrame loops / before-afters. Pure scroll-stopper. Carries the others.

## Post formulas (fill-in-the-blank)

**X — the "most people don't know" hook**
```
Most Wix sites don't know they can [capability].

[1 line of how].

[micro-result or screenshot].

(Built this into [App] → link in bio)
```

**X — build-in-public**
```
[blunt status update].

[1 honest detail — a number, a blocker, a win].

[1 line of what's next].
```

**LinkedIn — the story / insight (3-part)**
```
[Hook: a sharp observation about Wix/small-biz sites].

[2–4 short lines: the problem most owners/agencies hit, and why it matters.]

[The turn: what we built / learned. Name the app once, naturally.]

[Soft CTA: a question, or "happy to share how" — invite a comment, not a click.]
```

**Short-form video (DeepFrame — Reels/TikTok/Shorts)**
```
HOOK (0–2s, on-screen text): "Your Wix hero section is boring. Watch this."
SHOW (2–10s): the DeepFrame shader morphing behind a sample headline.
PAYOFF (10–15s): "Real-time. No video file. Works on any Wix site." + app name.
```

## Hook bank (steal these)

- "Most Wix sites are leaving [X] on the table."
- "I audited [N] Wix sites this month. The same 3 things were always broken." [confirm N]
- "Your Wix site loads fine for you. Here's why it doesn't for Google's AI." (SurfacedGEO)
- "Stop paying a developer for [thing the app does in 2 clicks]."
- "I submitted my [Nth] app to the Wix App Market. Here's what nobody tells you about review." (build-in-public)
- "Static hero images are a 2015 look. Here's the 2026 version." (DeepFrame)
- "Quizzes convert better than contact forms. Proof: ↓" (KnowIt) [confirm with a real stat or soften]

## Repurposing rules (1 idea → many posts)

- 1 LinkedIn story → 1 punchy X version (cut to the single sharpest line) → 1 quote-graphic (Canva).
- 1 DeepFrame clip → Reel + TikTok + X video + LinkedIn "look what's possible" post.
- Every winner (top ~10% by engagement) → mark evergreen → auto-reposts in 4–6 weeks.

## Honesty guardrail

Anything tagged **[confirm]** in `posts.md` is a claim I couldn't verify from the codebase —
replace with a real number or soften before posting. Don't ship invented stats.
